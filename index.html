Our Story

About Us

Kirti is an Endurance athlete who began her fitness journey at the age of 37 and has since inspired many through her achievements. Most notable being:

  • Multiple podium finishes in Marathons, Ultra-marathons and Triathlon events.
  • Bronze Medalist at National Swimming Competition.
  • Completed prestigious events including The Border Ultra 100km, Ultribe Paderu 50km, Patnitop 50km, Pinkathon 50km and 97 km in a 12-hour stadium run.
  • An experienced mountaineer and cyclist having participated in many events including BRMs.
  • Instrumental in conduct of various running events with leading running communities such as Pinkathon, Tar Blazers, and VTRA since 2019.

She is a passionate advocate for community fitness, women's participation in sports, and healthy lifestyles.

She sets the example that it is never too late to start your fitness journey and is dedicated to promote the message: "Fit Family, Happy Family."
